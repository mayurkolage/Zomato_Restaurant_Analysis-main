## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes 9,500+ restaurants listed on Zomato across 100+ countries to uncover insights around restaurant density, pricing, ratings, cuisines, and service features (online delivery, table booking). The workflow covers the full analytics pipeline:

1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation** — Excel
2. **Data Modeling & Analysis** — MySQL (SQL)
3. **Dashboarding & Visualization** — Power BI and Tableau

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

- Country-wise restaurant distribution — count, average rating, average cost, % of total
- Custom calendar table (year, month, quarter, financial month/quarter) from opening dates
- Currency standardization — converted local currencies to USD for fair cost comparison
- City & country restaurant counts
- Restaurant openings over time (by year, quarter, month)
- Rating distribution
- Price bucketing — Low / Medium / High / Luxury
- Table booking & online delivery adoption rates
- Top cuisines and top cities
- Top-rated cities (minimum 20 restaurants)
- Top 10 restaurants by votes
- Global KPIs — total restaurants, countries, cities, avg rating, total votes
